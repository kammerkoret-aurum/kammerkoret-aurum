Welcome to our anniversary concert!

Kammerkoret Aurum are celebrating their 20 year anniversary this autumn, and are happy to invite old and new audiences to our anniversary concert! The concert will demonstrate a cross section of our repertoire through the 20 years, and we are testing the width of choral music in genres and moods.\
\
The repertoire is varied with some pieces having been sung by the choir since 2006 while other pieces are new for both choir and audience. We are performing music by, amongst others, Eila Fjose, Jaakko Mäntyjärvi, Caroline Shaw, Torbjørn Dyrud, Rachmaninov, and of course something new and exciting from our own Eva Holm Foosnæs!

The concert consists of two parts with a short break, in which there will be some refreshments.
